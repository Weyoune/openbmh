## Diamond 3.7 foreign crests

The drawing policy is installed from the verified gate in the supplied executable.
Diamond 3.7 uses individual original W-sheet cells for the 48 foreign league clubs.
Earlier verified editions retain their neutral foreign-club crest. Database and
savegame selectors remain authoritative, including after club movement; no
high-resolution replacement pack or changed competition structure is required.
Unknown drawing gates retain the conservative legacy policy. National teams,
non-rostered clubs and missing-club tokens retain their existing treatment.

Private data verification decoded all 48 Diamond 3.7 selections and checked their
38-pixel cells against atlas bounds. This is an asset-path/contact-sheet check,
not a claim of completed interactive GUI acceptance.

bug corrections. The supplied original executable hash and all 1,084 documented
